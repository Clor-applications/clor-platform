## How it was checked

- `scripts/inventory-routes.mjs` resolves every identifier with the TypeScript compiler (imports and aliases), expands registration helpers at their call sites, mounts routers from `app.use(...)` in order, and applies router `.use(...)` only to routes registered after it.
- Spot checks: 14 random staff writes compared by hand with their source line (all matched); every route whose permission is found only in the handler (payments, recycle bin, stock documents) read in full.
- Limits: a check reached through a helper is credited even if the helper takes it on one branch only (branches decided by bound constants, such as `if (to === 'APPROVED')`, are followed exactly). Ownership checks inside services (such as `isOwner`) are not recognised as guards; they appear as allow-list entries with a finding.
