# clor.in hero clip, 3D (13 Nov 2026)

- **Message:** at month end, one pass runs through CLOR: People, then Attendance, then
  Payroll, then Books, with no spreadsheet in between.
- **Destination:** background of the clor.in home page hero, behind the headline.
- **Format:** 1920 x 1080, 10 s, seamless loop, no sound. Left ~44% kept calm and empty for
  the headline.
- **Look:** Azure-like premium 3D on the site's light ground. Real CLOR screens (drawn from
  the site's sample figures) floating in depth, joined by an orange line of light; glossy
  orange and blue glass ribbons and spheres behind. Orange #FF5400, navy ink #0f172a.
- **Runtime:** Three.js (HyperFrames `three` adapter), Plus Jakarta Sans for screen text.
- Owner asked for a hero video and for HyperFrames / Three.js; rejected generic 3D icon art.
