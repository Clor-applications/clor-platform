# Employee view audit (27 Oct 2026)

This audit follows an ordinary employee of a 1–50 person Indian business through Workforce, Organization and Time & Leave. It is read-only: code was read, nothing was changed. There are three ways in, and they do not offer the same things:

| Door | Who | Code |
|---|---|---|
| **Web employee app** (`#/portal`) | Staff with no CLOR sign-in (most of them) | `apps/attendance/src/portal/EmployeePortal.jsx`, `MyDetails.jsx`, `portalApi.js`; server `server/src/routes/portal.ts`, `peoplePortal.ts` (mounted at `/portal/people`) |
| **Native Android app** | The same staff, on Android | `native/attendance-android/app/src/main/java/in/clor/attendance/` (`data/Api.kt`, `ui/*.kt`) |
| **People web "Me"** | Staff who also have a CLOR sign-in | `apps/people/manifest.jsx:248` (`Me` = `myLeave`, `myDays`); `packages/ui/src/timeOff/MyDays.jsx`, `apps/attendance/src/screens/MyLeave.jsx` |

The main finding: **the server does far more than any one screen shows.** Payslips, month review, overtime, the "what needs fixing" list, recent decisions, resigning, the help-desk thread and the private complaint all have employee routes. The web app leaves out half of them, the Android app leaves out the other half, and some appear in neither.

### Mobile fit at 375px (web app)

- The shell is phone-first: `max-w-md`, one column, `px-4`, a fixed bottom nav with 5–6 equal tabs at 11px (`:148-169`), and `pb-20` keeps content clear of the nav. Six tabs (approvers) is about 62px each, which is tight for "Approvals" but fits.
- The **Me tab is 12 stacked cards** (`:145`, `:1006-1027`): job, details, requests, policies, Ask HR, identity, tax, documents, data, devices, password, sign out. Documents (the 8th card) and the manager (the 1st card) are hard to find. This needs a sub-menu.
- The My days table (`:372-409`) has 4 columns inside `overflow-x-auto ui-table-fit`. It fits, but with no gap to spare.
- Two-column date inputs (`grid-cols-2 gap-3`, `:900`, `:539`) are about 160px each, which is fine on Android Chrome; iOS Safari's date control is wider and may clip.
- Several targets are small text links: `ui-link text-xs` Change / Cancel / Came back early (`:939-942`) and "How is this worked out?" (`:878`). They are well under 44px on a busy row.
- Hard-coded `#64748b` for a missing shift colour (`:637`) breaks the token rule (DESIGN.md).

## 2. Ten biggest gaps, ranked

1. **Payslips are missing from the web employee app (P0).** The server serves them (`portal.ts:1294`) and Android shows them. Every iPhone and desktop employee has none. **Fix:** add `getPayslips`/`getPayslip` to `portalApi.js` and a "Payslips" card at the top of Me (list of period, net, Paid; open shows lines; Save PDF, built the way `Payslips.kt` does it). Show it only when `available`.

2. **Decisions do not reach portal-only employees (P1).** `leave_decided`, request decisions and the lapse warning need a `userId` (`leave.ts:1003`, `leaveJobs.ts:111`). **Fix:** have the web app call `/portal/updates` on load and every few minutes while open. Show a "What's new" strip on Today (approved/rejected with the note), mark items seen by `key`, and send the lapse notice through the same feed. Later, add SMS/WhatsApp for decisions.

3. **Resigning has a server and no screen (P1).** **Fix:** add a "Leaving the company" row at the bottom of Me that opens a form with last working day (notice period pre-filled from terms), reason and confirm, posting `/portal/people/me/resign`. Once sent, show `/me/leaving` (waiting, approved last day) under My job. Put the same thing in Android.

4. **Android and web disagree on what the employee can do (P1).** Android has payslips, month review, overtime, Needs attention, forgot-password and the next holidays. The web has WFH/Permission, leave preview, edit leave, came back early, restricted holidays, My details, policies, Ask HR and tax. **Fix:** write one capability matrix into TIME-LEAVE-DECISIONS ("Employee app" row) and close it both ways. First the web gets #1, month review and attention. Then Android gets leave preview (`/leave/preview`), the WFH/Permission kinds (`/requests`), Change leave and My details.

5. **Android "Update my details" files a DPDP data-rights correction (P1).** `RequestsTab.kt:324`. HR's data-protection queue gets profile edits on a legal 30-day clock, and the structured change request is bypassed. **Fix:** point Android at `/portal/people/me/details` + `/me/change-request` with the same fields as `MyDetails.jsx`. Keep CORRECTION for real data-rights requests only.

6. **Ask HR cannot hold a conversation (P1).** The web app uses the one-shot `/hr-cases`, while the threaded `/portal/people/desk*` routes are unused. **Fix:** switch `AskHr` to `/desk`. Opening a case shows its messages (internal notes are already excluded, schema `internal`), with a reply box and Reopen. Badge cases where HR wrote last.

7. **There is no private complaint channel (P1).** `/portal/people/private-cases` has no screen. **Fix:** add a quiet "Raise a private complaint" link under Me → Help (not on Today). Show a short form, then only the reference and the inquiry-due date, as the route returns. Do not list it back, by design.

8. **Bank account change has no safe path (P1).** **Fix:** once Payroll decides the approval rule (TRACKER:61), add "Salary account" to My details as an *always-to-HR* field with IFSC lookup and a masked current number. Until then, show a Pay card in Me saying "To change your salary account, ask HR", pre-filling an Ask HR case in the Pay category.

9. **Failures look like "nothing here" (P2).** `MyDocuments`, `Policies`, `AskHr` and `MyTax` catch errors and render nothing (`:1325`, `MyDetails.jsx:221`, `:1554`, `:1120`); documents use `alert()`; `CompanyUpdates` shows a raw `e.message` (`:1537`). **Fix:** give each card a one-line "Could not load your documents. Try again" using the existing `LoadFailed`. Replace `alert` and `window.confirm` (`:1454`) with `notify`/`confirmDialog`. Add a confirm to leave Cancel (`:783`).

10. **Holidays and who is away are thin (P2).** **Fix:** add a "Holidays {year}" list (all of the person's area holidays, with restricted ones marked and Pick inline) as a section of Leave, served from `loadHolidays` by a `/portal/holidays?year=`. In the leave preview, add a count with no names: "2 others in your department are away on some of these days" (the server already computes `sameDepartment`; pass a count-only variant when `req` is null).

Close behind these: the Me tab needs to be split into sub-screens (Profile, Pay & tax, Documents, Help, Account, Privacy). The People-web "Me" for signed-in staff should get My details, Documents and Payslips, so the owner and staff with a sign-in never need the second password. Add a Today/Tomorrow chip on the leave first-day field. The "No check-out" pill should open the missed-punch form prefilled.

## 3. What is genuinely good (vs Keka, greytHR, Zoho People)

Grounded in `TIME-LEAVE-MARKET-RESEARCH.md` §(a), §(c).

- **Date-by-date leave preview before Send** (`LeavePreview`, `:705-732`). Each skipped date comes with its reason (weekly off, holiday name, sandwich), the balance after, the split across leave periods spelled out, and problems in plain words, all before saving. The research found nothing like it in public material; Keka, greytHR and Zoho show a balance, not the dates (§(c)1).
- **"How is this worked out?"** on every paid balance (`LeaveExplain`), and **"Why my day says this"** on every attendance day (`DayExplain`, `:401`). Competitors' employee apps show the number; CLOR shows the ledger behind it. That targets the #2 and #10 user complaints in TIME-LEAVE-DECISIONS ("Wrong balances fixed by hand", "Balances hard to read").
- **Came back early** (`:828-846`). Keka lets employees cancel only *future* approved leave (§(c)5). Here the person shortens leave already in progress and is charged only for the days taken.
- **Change a waiting leave or WFH request** with the same preview, which knows about its own days (`ownPreview`, `:692`). Users list "no edit after applying" as a top complaint (rank 8).
- **WFH, On duty and Permission as present, not leave,** with the month's quota shown as "2 of 4 used, 1 waiting, 1 left" (`:442`, `:567`). This matches Keka, beats greytHR's public material, and is clearer than Zoho's On-duty catch-all (§(a)3).
- **Private leave** (`reasonNeeded = !type.isPrivate`, `:743`). The reason is optional and the manager sees it masked (`:1285`). Not found elsewhere (§(c)2).
- **Restricted holidays picked by the employee,** with "N of M picked" (`:962-979`).
- **Plain words throughout.** "Sent for approval.", "It waits for a decision again.", "Nothing can be asked for right now.", "Waiting / Approved / Rejected", "No check-out". Errors come from the server in the same voice (`portal.ts:920`). This beats the "Regularization", "LOP" and "Comp-off" jargon of greytHR and Keka screens.
- **Self-service details with clear rules:** contact fields save at once, the rest go to HR with "decide within N working days", and HR's question can be answered in the app (`MyDetails.jsx:117-121`, `:201-206`). "My job" (`:58-79`) shows probation end, contract end and reporting manager, which Zoho shows only in profile tabs.
- **A manager's approval on the phone,** with a mandatory, visible reject reason (`:1287-1294`) and private leave masked. This closes market-research G8 for portal managers.
- **DPDP built in:** the notice is read and recorded, and the employee can download their data, withdraw consent per purpose, nominate someone, or raise a grievance with an answer-by date (`:1393-1527`). No Indian competitor's public material shows this for employees.
- **Signed-in devices with remote sign-out** (`:1037-1089`), and a password change that signs out other phones.
- **The Android app is a real native app,** not a wrapper: Material 3, a Keystore-encrypted token, permissions asked only when needed, on-device reminders, payslip PDF generated on the phone (`README.md`). Its Home (month figures, week chart, next holidays, team away for managers) is closer to Keka's mobile home than the web app's Today is.
