# Get the manager and the place right first

For an Indian business with 1–100 employees, the Organization module succeeds or fails on two records, not on hierarchy depth. The first is **each person's reporting manager**, which silently drives approvals and the org chart. The second is **each person's place of work, keyed to a state**, which drives Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund, holidays, Shops & Establishments or OSH Code rules and minimum wages. Every competitor, Indian or global, ships the same small core of department, designation (job title), location and manager. Legal entities, business units, divisions, cost centres, grades, teams and positions are layers added for larger or multi-company customers, and the products aimed at small firms hide them until switched on. The user complaint with the best evidence is a **missing or unmapped manager that blocks an employee's resignation, expense claim or confirmation**, seen across greytHR, ZingHR and PeopleStrong. Holiday lists that are missing or stale come next. Admin-side pain such as renames that break history, bulk moves and messy imports is thinly evidenced because Reddit, G2 and Capterra could not be read. India adds one modelling rule that matters more than any hierarchy: GST "branch", labour-law "establishment" and payroll "work location" are three registers kept by three regulators. Store them as attributes of one place record with a state, not as three object types. The recommended build is department, designation, place, manager, a dated per-person job history, an audit trail for structure changes, and data-quality checks. Everything else should stay behind a switch or be left out.

**How to read the evidence labels in this report.** *Documented* means a vendor help-centre page that was read in full. *Help-centre summary* means the page was blocked and only a search engine's summary of that exact URL was seen. *Marketing* means a vendor sales page. *Third-party* means API directories, resellers, consultancies or competitor blogs. *User evidence* means a dated review or community post by a real user. Coverage is uneven. Zoho People, greytHR, Personio, Factorial and Gusto have readable documentation. Keka, HiBob, Deel and BambooHR were seen mostly through summaries. Darwinbox, factoHR, HROne, Freshteam and Sage HR are close to undocumented in public, and their gaps below mean "not found", not "absent".

## Every vendor ships the same four objects; the rest is enterprise layering

The shared vocabulary in Indian products is **Department, Designation, Location and Grade**. Global small-business tools converge on **department (one per person, optionally nested), location, job title and manager** ([Gusto, help-centre summary](https://support.gusto.com/article/100011863100000/Set-up-and-manage-departments); [Personio, help-centre summary](https://support.personio.de/hc/en-us/articles/18862110441885-Set-up-departments-and-teams)). The vendors split into three modelling philosophies. **Keka** imposes a fixed stack of legal entity, then business unit, then department with sub-departments, plus location, pay grades and pay bands. It asks for a legal entity before anything else ([Keka, help-centre summary](https://help.keka.com/hc/en-us/articles/39946699284625); [Keka setup](https://help.keka.com/admin/setting-up-the-organizational-structure)). **Zoho People** keeps departments, which can have a parent, separate from an optional "Organization Structure" of legal entity, business unit and division. That structure is off until enabled and its labels can be renamed. Zoho states plainly that "departments cannot be tagged to organization structure entities" ([Zoho People: Organization Structure, documented](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/people/administrator-guide/settings/manage-accounts/articles/organization-structure-zoho-people)). **greytHR** has no structure model at all. Department, designation, grade and location are all flat "Employee Categories", admins can add any number of them, and greytHR's own help warns admins to contact support before changing the settings ([greytHR categories, documented](https://www.greythr.com/help-admin/employee-settings/setup-employee-categories-guide/); [greytHR Category Group](https://admin-help.greythr.com/admin/answers/KV9heDtzRFKjindvFuShQQ)). Deel and HiBob take a fourth route of generic, admin-defined "structures" or "lists". Deel pre-enables Department and lets admins make any added structure mandatory on every contract ([Deel, help-centre summary](https://help.letsdeel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/24441578301073-How-to-Configure-Organization-Structure-and-Worker-Relations)).

The higher layers sort cleanly by customer size. **True position management, meaning seats that exist and can sit vacant without a person in them, appears only in enterprise products.** Darwinbox exposes Position, Cost Center and Business Unit masters through its API ([Knit API directory, third-party](https://getknit.dev/blog/darwinbox-api-directory-QrXQRZ)). Sage People has budgeted positions, but a third-party source mixes it up with Sage HR, so the small-business product should not be credited with them ([Cleverence, third-party](https://www.cleverence.com/articles/sage-documentation/about-employee-positions-sage-5926/)). Cost centres are documented only for Darwinbox and for Deel Global Payroll, where assigning them is restricted to admin roles ([Deel, help-centre summary](https://help.letsdeel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/26165580703633-Global-Payroll-Entity-Level-Permissions)). Teams exist for matrix organisations. Personio puts it best: departments are always required, but teams only "if your company uses a matrix structure" ([Personio](https://support.personio.de/hc/en-us/articles/18862110441885-Set-up-departments-and-teams)). Smaller Indian players confirm the core. Kredily has departments with sub-departments, a Department Head import column and grades nested under designations ([Kredily, documented](https://kredily.com/support/company/departments-designations-grades.html)). sumHR lists Designation, Teams, Department, office locations, Business Unit and Grades in its help ([sumHR, titles only](https://sumhr.helpscoutdocs.com/category/148-company-data)).

Two takeaways shape the build. First, **department heads are labels almost everywhere**. Approvals follow the reporting manager or role-based rules, not the head ([Zoho, documented](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/people/administrator-guide/settings/manage-accounts/articles/organization-structure-zoho-people); [Gusto manager permissions](https://support.gusto.com/article/145232124100000/Manager-permission-types)). The exception is Zoho, where approval steps can target "Department Lead" or "Department Head" ([Zoho approvals, documented](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/people/administrator-guide/common-settings/articles/approvals-overview-use-cases)). So in Zoho a wrong head quietly changes who approves. Personio removes that ambiguity by saying leads "only appear in the org chart and don't affect other parts of Personio" ([Personio, help-centre summary](https://support.personio.de/hc/en-us/articles/29762795544989-Manage-the-Org-chart)). Second, **no vendor documents a department-tree chart among the Indian products**. Personio's three-way chart (by people, departments or teams, with member counts, leads, search and export that respects filters) is the most complete documented small-business chart ([Personio Org chart overview](https://support.personio.de/hc/en-us/articles/360017540757-Overview-of-the-Org-chart)). greytHR is the best Indian example of the chart as an editing surface ([greytHR Organization Chart, documented](https://www.greythr.com/help-admin/core-hr/organization-chart-manager-assignment/)).

## Restructuring happens to people, never to the structure

Across all fourteen vendors, **effective dating lives on the employee, not on the department**. BambooHR adds a new dated row to the employee's job table whenever department, division, location, title or manager changes ([BambooHR API, documented](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/reference/update-employee)). HiBob does the same in its Work table, and its Audit Report records who changed what, with an effective date that can differ from the date of entry ([HiBob Audit Report](https://help.hibob.com/en/articles/3651065-audit-report)). Keka's bulk Employee Job Details Import accepts **future effective dates** for department, location, manager, pay band, pay grade and job title, and applies them on the day ([Keka release notes, help-centre summary](https://help.keka.com/hc/en-us/articles/50139600428817-Release-Notes-Future-Effective-Dates-for-Bulk-Employee-Job-Changes)). greytHR keeps Position History cards with from and to dates per category ([greytHR, documented](https://admin-help.greythr.com/admin/answers/121730636/)). No vendor documents a structure-level event such as "Sales splits into two from 1 April", and **no vendor documents merging two departments**.

Retirement is where the products differ most, and the honest answer is that most leave it undocumented. Kredily blocks deleting a department or designation while people are assigned to it ([Kredily](https://kredily.com/support/company/departments-designations-grades.html)). HiBob's list values carry an `archived` flag, so retiring a value keeps its history ([HiBob API](https://apidocs.hibob.com/docs/fields-metadata)). Gusto lets admins rename or remove departments freely, but it treats **locations as tax records**. It tells admins not to delete old work addresses "if employees ever worked there", blocks edits to an address with active employees, and retires a location by moving people to a new one from a start date ([Gusto: Edit company details, help-centre summary](https://support.gusto.com/article/106622257100000/Edit-company-details)). That rule transfers directly to India, where the state of the workplace sets Professional Tax. Renaming in place is the common route everywhere, and it has a known cost: a data-cleaning vendor reports that BambooHR department renames are not applied to historical records, which breaks trend analysis ([dataclean.to, vendor-adjacent](https://dataclean.to/use-cases/clean-bamboohr-for-analytics)). Future-dated rows cause their own trouble. BambooHR's NetSuite sync skips employees whose Division change is future-dated ([BambooHR Marketplace](https://www.bamboohr.com/integrations/listings/netsuite-employee-sync)), and the API hides future rows unless asked ([BambooHR API](https://documentation.bamboohr.com/reference/get-employee)).

Import and data quality are a clear open gap. Keka, Zoho, HiBob, Personio and Kredily all import structure or job data, and Factorial requires a separate import per legal entity ([Factorial importer, help-centre summary](https://help.factorialhr.com/en_US/managing-my-company-account/about-the-employee-importer)). Old BambooHR partner guidance demanded headers that exactly match field names and one table per sheet ([BambooHR partner doc, about 2015](https://partners.bamboohr.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/04/New-Import-Documentation.doc)). **No vendor documents an audit log of the structure itself** (who created, renamed or re-headed a department), and none documents data-quality checks for people without a manager, empty departments or departments without a head. HROne's advertised audit trail covers policy changes only ([HROne Core HR, marketing](https://hrone.cloud/hr-software/core-hr/)). Setup is presented everywhere as settings masters, with no documented guided wizard. The good small-business pattern is "off until needed": Gusto departments must be enabled, BambooHR's directory and chart are off by default, and Gusto lets an admin create a department inline from an employee's profile ([Gusto, help-centre summary](https://support.gusto.com/hc/en-us/articles/220382007)).

## Users report the manager link breaking, not missing hierarchy features

The evidence base is weak, so the ranking below is a judgement on harm to a 1–100 person firm combined with how often each problem appears. **Reddit was unreachable by every route tried, and G2 and Capterra returned 403 errors.** The user evidence therefore comes from about 13,000 Google Play and about 1,000 India App Store reviews of Indian HR apps, plus scattered community threads. App-store reviewers are almost all employees, not the owners who build the structure, and org structure is rarely their subject: attendance, GPS, login and speed dominate. Admin-side pain such as bulk moves, renames, duplicate lists and import is **close to unrepresented**. Treat its absence as a hole in the data, not as proof the problem is rare.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missing or unmapped reporting manager blocks resignation, claims and confirmations, with no clear message | High: the employee is stuck and cannot fix it | **Medium (strongest in set)**: 4 independent reviews across 3 vendors, plus troubleshooting articles from 3 more | User evidence + documented |
| 2 | Pending requests stay with the old manager after a manager change | High | Low–medium: 1 vendor doc, 1 review of an approval stuck at "L1 pending" | Documented + user evidence |
| 3 | A strict approval tree cannot express small-firm reality: two founders approving each other, an owner with no manager | High for 1–20 people | Low: 1 Zoho thread | User evidence |
| 4 | Holiday list missing, not shown or not updated for the new year | Medium: staff mark attendance on holidays | Medium: about 7 mentions, 3 vendors, mostly before 2023 | User evidence |
| 5 | Wrong designation printed on payslips and impossible to correct afterwards | Medium: statutory documents carry it | Low: 2 mentions + 1 display regression | User evidence |
| 6 | Strict branch mapping blocks staff who work across sites | Medium for multi-site firms | Low: 2 mentions | User evidence |

The top complaint is concrete. A greytHR user in January 2025 could not submit a resignation for a week because "my manager's name is not reflecting in my profile" ([Google Play, greytHR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greytip.ghress&reviewId=3576c0ad-643b-4ad3-aa4a-4586ddcee91b)). A ZingHR user said the app "hasn't yet mapped my manager for the resignation" ([Google Play, ZingHR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zinghr.app&reviewId=e8d48a45-6efa-4d2d-ac48-ce612472be4e)). A PeopleStrong user could not file a reimbursement because L1 manager details were missing and could not be added ([Google Play, PeopleStrong](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.peoplestrong.alt.organise&reviewId=d5ec4017-cfa4-43c9-9946-18eec6809208)). Vendors publish articles titled "My reporting manager in the org chart is incorrect" ([Quikchex](https://qcknowledgebasehelpdocs.quikchex.in/article/39-my-reporting-manager-in-the-org-chart-is-incorrect-how-do-i-update-it)), which is indirect evidence that the problem is common. Kredily documents that after a manager change, requests "already pending may still sit with the previous approver" ([Kredily](https://kredily.com/support/employee/directory-and-reporting-manager.html)). The small-firm edge case comes from a Zoho admin whose "top two exec approve each others leave requests". Zoho would not let them report to each other, and a custom rule did not route correctly ([Zoho community](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/need-leave-approvals-to-go-to-someone-other-than-reporting-to-department?page=14)). Designation errors matter because designations print onto documents. A greytHR user was told a wrong designation on a payslip could not be fixed once the payslip was generated ([Google Play, greytHR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greytip.ghress&reviewId=f1b96ef8-a873-4352-b6d8-084e14c56b17)). Holiday complaints are about **visibility and staleness, not wrong-branch rules**. No review in the sample reported another location's holidays being applied ([Google Play, ZingHR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zinghr.app&reviewId=1392c11f-2391-434d-9d63-4f02f4ad9614); [Google Play, greytHR](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.greytip.ghress&reviewId=c879ff12-dc14-45ff-8fa9-27303902808d)).

On the positive side, employees praise one thing: seeing the hierarchy. Keka users value "hierarchy... at finger tips" ([Google Play, Keka](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.keka.xhr&reviewId=b8db418b-f34d-44aa-9e76-bfe48c1ffcaf)). A Darwinbox user asked for an "organogram with names for designation" so new joiners understand the company ([Google Play, Darwinbox](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.darwinbox.darwinbox&reviewId=182b501d-47d6-4a2c-a90b-66a85ddb4f8b)). The Zoho One user who has to maintain departments "in both Zoho One and Zoho People" called it "time-consuming and prone to errors" ([Zoho Community](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/community/topic/automatic-department-and-employee-sync-between-zoho-one-and-zoho-people)). That complaint is a warning for any suite that runs accounting and HR side by side.

## India keys almost every place rule to the state of the workplace

Three regulators keep three different registers, and none of them is simply "the office". **GST registers the legal entity once per state**. Within a state, one GSTIN covers a principal place of business plus additional places of business ([CBIC GST registration flier](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/pdf/e-version-gst-fliers/regn-GST-onlineversion-07june2017.pdf)). **Labour law registers each establishment (premises)**, and in 2026 that registration is moving from state Shops & Establishments Acts to the OSH Code at different speeds in each state. Maharashtra, Haryana, Karnataka and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands exempted OSH-registered establishments from separate S&E registration. Maharashtra and Karnataka still require establishments **under 10 workers** to give a simple intimation. Bihar repealed its S&E Act. The guidance for Karnataka is to keep S&E compliance until OSH registration is operational there ([Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas ELB Newsletter, Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)). **Payroll products key compliance to the employee's "work location"**. Zoho Payroll's work location has five fields (name, address, city, state, PIN), the setup address becomes an undeletable primary location, and home workers get their home added as a location ([Zoho Payroll help](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/help/employer/settings/set-work-locations.md)). Frappe HR instead stores an "Employment State" on each salary assignment ([Frappe HR docs](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/india-payroll/professional-tax)).

The state is the load-bearing field. **Professional Tax follows the recorded workplace**, not the registered office or the employee's home, and remote work does not change it until the official location is updated ([Treelife PT guide](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)). One source says PT follows the registered office ([Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/professional-tax-india-state-wise-rates-slabs)), but Treelife, Zoho and Frappe all key it to the work state. PT registration is per state. **Odisha abolished PT from 1 April 2026**, and Karnataka revised its slab from April 2025 ([Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)), which is why rate tables must be dated data rather than code. LWF is state-run, with 16 jurisdictions in Zoho's list, each with its own amounts and frequency, computed "based on your work location" ([Zoho LWF guide](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/taxes-and-compliance/guide-to-lwf.html)). Sources conflict on Delhi and on current amounts. EPF is national, with one code for the company, while ESI branches typically need a sub-code per region. That ESI point rests only on forum and old-circular evidence ([greytHR community](https://community.greythr.com/t/esic-pf-for-different-branches-in-india/1913); [Zoho multi-state payroll](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/taxes-and-compliance/multi-state-payroll-in-india.html)). Minimum wages follow the state and zone under a national floor set by the Code on Wages ([KPMG India](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)).

Holidays follow state law for each establishment. A set of mandatory national holidays is combined with festival holidays that the employer picks from the state list. Karnataka requires at least 10 days a year ([greytHR wiki on the 1963 Act](https://greythr.com/wiki/acts/karnataka-industrial-establishments-national-and-festival-holidays-act-1963)). Maharashtra reportedly requires 4 national plus 4 festival holidays ([Simpliance](https://www.simpliance.in/nfh_detail/maharashtra)). Zoho Payroll attaches each holiday to the work locations where it applies ([Zoho Payroll holidays](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/help/employer/leave-and-attendance/holiday.html)). Restricted or optional holidays look like a company-policy device rather than a statutory duty for private employers, but no source confirmed this. Two more facts tie places to documents and to events. The Labour Codes (in force from 21 November 2025, with Central Rules notified on 8 May 2026) require appointment letters for everyone, and several notified OSH Rules prescribe a format that includes **establishment details** ([AMS Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf); [KPMG India](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)). And when an employee moves from Karnataka to Maharashtra, PT, LWF, the holiday calendar, the governing state rules and the minimum-wage schedule all switch on the move date, and the employer may need new state registrations ([Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)). Which states have final Code rules is contested: AMS's October list excludes Karnataka and Maharashtra from OSH rules, while a May blog claimed otherwise. Hard-coding "which Act applies where" in 2026 would therefore be a mistake.

## What to build, what to switch off, and what to call it

The model that follows from this evidence is small and opinionated. Its depth goes into change over time and the India place rules, not into extra layers. CLOR already has a platform-level `Branch` record with state, GSTIN, GST registration type and an active flag, which accounting uses (`server/prisma/platform/schema.prisma`). The single most useful decision is to **make that record the employee's place of work too**, rather than creating a separate HR location list. This avoids the double-maintenance trap the Zoho One user described, and it lets one place carry a GSTIN, an establishment registration and a state at the same time.

| Decision | What | Why (evidence) |
|---|---|---|
| **Build** | Place of work = the shared Branch record. Add establishment registration type (S&E / OSH Code / intimation only / none) and number as plain owner-entered data, and a head-office flag | Three regulators, one premises; registration law is in flux in 2026 (AMS) |
| **Build** | Archive-only places once anyone has worked there; "move people here from date"; never edit the address while people are assigned (correct vs change as separate actions) | Gusto's tax-history rule; PT follows the recorded workplace |
| **Build** | Reporting manager on everyone; the owner is the default and the only person allowed none; block cycles; show "approvals go to X" on the profile and before submit; on manager change, offer to move pending requests | Rank 1–3 problems; Deel blocks loops; Kredily pending-request gap |
| **Build** | Named approver override per person (covers co-founders approving each other, deputies) | Zoho thread: a pure tree cannot express it |
| **Build** | Department: optional, flat by default, nesting allowed, one per person; create inline from the employee form | Gusto, Personio; owner-run firms often need none |
| **Build** | Department head as a **display label**, stated plainly on screen, never a hidden approver or data grant | Personio wording; Zoho's head-as-approver makes wrong heads silently re-route |
| **Build** | Designation, effective-dated; payslips and letters read the value as of their own date | greytHR payslip complaint; Codes require appointment letters |
| **Build** | Dated job history per person (department, designation, place, manager) separating effective date from entry date; future-dated moves with a clear "becomes current on" rule | BambooHR, HiBob, Keka; BambooHR future-row sync gotcha |
| **Build** | Structure audit trail (created, renamed, head changed, merged, retired), rename keeps the same ID, explicit merge and retire-with-people-moved | No vendor documents structure audit or merge; rename breaks history at BambooHR |
| **Build** | Checks: nobody without a manager, empty departments, places missing a state, **first person in a new state** with a PT/LWF/ESI/establishment checklist, next year's holidays not set | Open gap at every vendor; India state triggers; stale holiday complaints |
| **Build** | Holiday calendar per state per year, with a per-place override; owner picks festival days from the state list | Karnataka and Maharashtra Acts; Zoho per-location holidays |
| **Build** | Org chart from the manager field, with a department view, search, back-navigation, PNG/PDF export, visible to employees on mobile | Personio pattern; BambooHR and Rippling user asks; employees value seeing the hierarchy |
| **Build** | CSV import with tolerant header matching and a preview of errors before commit | BambooHR exact-header friction |
| **Behind a switch** | Legal entity (only for owners running more than one company); grades; teams | Keka's entity-first setup is ceremony for 10 people; Zoho and Gusto opt-in pattern |
| **Leave out** | Positions or vacant seats, business units, divisions, cost centres in HR, dotted-line managers, department-scoped permissions, headcount planning, any mandatory extra dimension | Enterprise-only (Darwinbox, Sage People, Rippling); Keka's required "Business Function" adds friction; Darwinbox rigidity complaints |

Naming should follow how Indian owners and Indian products talk. Use **Department** and **Designation**, since "designation" is the Indian word for job title and appears on payslips. Use **Grade** only once switched on. For the place, two labels compete. "Branch" is GST and accounting vocabulary, "establishment" is labour-law vocabulary, and "Work location" is what Zoho Payroll shows. Because CLOR's place is the accounting Branch record, keep **Branch** as the object name, label the employee field **"Works at"**, and show registrations as plain attributes ("GSTIN", "Shops & Establishments / OSH registration"). Do not introduce "establishment" or "work site" as separate objects. Call the person **Reporting manager**, which is the term Indian employees used in the complaints, and avoid "L1". Avoid Keka's "Business unit" and Zoho's "Division" and "Stream" entirely.

## Conclusion

The research changes where the effort should go. Competitors spend their complexity on layers above the department, such as entities, business units, divisions and positions, that a 10-person firm never fills in. The pain users actually report sits below the department, in the manager link and in dated facts about a person. The most defensible differentiation for a small-business product is unglamorous: a manager link that can never be blank, approvals that say where they will go, history that survives renames, and a place record that knows its state. No Indian competitor documents structure-level audit, merge or data-quality checks, and none was found tying a "first employee in a new state" event to a compliance checklist. Those are open ground.

The biggest open question is the admin's voice. Every user complaint here comes from employees, and the problems owners hit while restructuring (renames, bulk moves, imports, duplicate lists) are inferred from vendor documentation rather than observed. A short round of direct Reddit and Capterra reading, or five interviews with owners who run HR themselves, would test the Build list before the history and merge features take more engineering time. On the India side, PT, LWF and holiday rules should be dated data that someone maintains, because Odisha's repeal and the shifting 2026 OSH notifications show the rules will keep moving under the product.
