# India place concepts for small-business HRMS: which rules follow which place (as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: India only. Researched 26 Oct 2026. Most 2026 status facts come from one law-firm newsletter (Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas ELB Newsletter, 1 Oct 2026) plus Big-4 alerts. Payroll-vendor blogs (Zoho, Treelife, greytHR) are used only as secondary sources and are flagged. Anything still pending notification is marked **[PENDING]**. Anything uncertain is marked **[UNCERTAIN]**.

## 1. Branch (GST) vs establishment (S&E / OSH / Factories) vs work location vs head office: how they differ, overlap and are named

### Takeaway
These are three different registers kept by three different regulators, and none of them is "the office". GST registers a **legal entity per state** (one GSTIN per PAN per state), with a principal place of business and additional places under it. Labour law registers each **establishment**, meaning premises where people work. That registration is moving in 2026 from state Shops & Establishments (S&E) Acts to OSH Code registration. Payroll products key PT, LWF and holidays to the employee's **work location**, which is a simple address-and-state record. For a 10–100 person company these usually collapse into one or two physical premises, but the model should keep "GST registration", "establishment registration" and "work location" as separate attributes of a place, not separate place types.

### Cited Findings
- A PAN-based legal entity has one GSTIN per state. Within a state, a single registration covers several branches: one place is declared the principal place of business and the others are additional places of business. — [CBIC GST registration flier](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/pdf/e-version-gst-fliers/regn-GST-onlineversion-07june2017.pdf)
- Section 25(2) of the CGST Act allows separate registrations for multiple places of business in the same state, under conditions (Rule 11). The usual route is still to add the premises as an additional place of business under the existing GSTIN through the amendment process. — [VJM Global](https://www.vjmglobal.com/feeds/blog/gst-registration-branch-office-another-state); [Kanakkupillai](https://www.kanakkupillai.com/learn/multiple-gst-registration-in-the-same-state/)
- On 10 Sep 2026 GSTN announced a facility that pre-fills details when an entity that already holds an active GSTIN under the same PAN applies for another registration, in the same or a different state. — [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/?p=1084759) (secondary; reported via search summary, not fetched)
- Labour registration is per establishment (premises). In 2026, Haryana (notification), Maharashtra (circular; dated 30 Apr / 4 May 2026), Karnataka (S&E Amendment Act 2026, reported 4 Sep 2026) and the Andaman & Nicobar Islands (12 Aug 2026) exempted **OSH Code-registered establishments** from separate S&E registration. Other S&E obligations (hours, leave, conditions of service) still apply where they are not inconsistent with the Code. — [Shardul Amarchand Mangaldas ELB Newsletter, Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- In Maharashtra, separate S&E registration is not required for establishments with **10 or more employees** once OSH Code registration is obtained. Establishments with **fewer than 10** must still give intimation of commencement under Section 7 of the Maharashtra S&E Act 2017. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- Karnataka's S&E (Amendment) Act 2026 takes establishments with 10+ workers that are registered under the OSH Code out of the S&E Act. Firms under 10 make a simple online intimation instead of registering. The Act also brings lifetime-valid registration, mandatory digital filing, a 7-day deadline for notifying changes, service certificates within 7 days, and a ban on keeping original employee documents. The firm's advice is that employers **keep existing S&E compliance until OSH Code registration is operational in Karnataka** [PENDING]. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- Bihar repealed its S&E Act (Ordinance first, then the Repeal Act 2026; dated 28 May / 23 Jul 2026). Assam amended its 2022 S&E Act on 2 Sep 2026, including online intimation for establishments with fewer than 10 workers. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- Some notified OSH Rules provide for deemed registration: the certificate is auto-generated if it is not issued within 7 days of a complete application. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- How products name it: Zoho Payroll uses **"Work Location"**, with only five fields: Work Location Name, Address, City, State, PIN Code. The address entered at organisation setup becomes the undeletable **primary work location**. Home-based employees get their home added as a separate work location. A work location is chosen when the employee is created. — [Zoho Payroll help: set work locations](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/help/employer/settings/set-work-locations.md)
- Frappe HR does not key PT to branch or company address. It uses an **"Employment State"** field on each Salary Structure Assignment, so a mid-year transfer gets a new assignment with a new state. — [Frappe HR docs: Professional Tax](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/india-payroll/professional-tax)

### Inferences
- A sensible model for small businesses is one **Place** entity (name, address, state, PIN) with optional attributes: *is head office / registered office*, *GSTIN link (principal or additional place)*, *establishment registration (OSH Code / S&E number, or "intimation only" if under 10 workers)*. Each employee has one current work place, with an effective-dated history. "Branch" is GST and accounting vocabulary. "Establishment" is labour-law vocabulary. "Work location" is what payroll vendors show users. Using "Work location" (or "Place of work") in HR screens matches Zoho and payroll convention, while GST-linked places can still be labelled "Branch" in the accounting side.
- The state is the load-bearing field: PT, LWF, S&E or OSH state rules, holidays and minimum wage all key off it. The city or zone matters only for minimum wages.
- Home and remote workers need a place record too, because Zoho treats a home address as a work location and PT follows the recorded workplace (see §2).
- Establishment registration is in flux by state during 2026. Store the registration *type* (S&E / OSH Code / intimation / none) as free data, not as fixed logic.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch the statutory definition of "place of business" (CGST Act s.2(85)) or the head-office vs registered-office distinction under the Companies Act. These are from memory and not cited.
- I found no source on how Keka or greytHR name these concepts (both are believed to use "Location"/"Legal entity", unverified).
- The Factories Act / OSH Code "factory" licence was not researched. It is probably irrelevant for 10–100 person service businesses.
- Whether Karnataka's OSH Code registration portal is live as of Oct 2026 was not confirmed.

## 2. Which rules follow which place (PT, LWF, S&E, holidays, ESI/PF, minimum wages)

### Takeaway
PT, LWF, S&E or OSH state rules, festival holidays and minimum wages all follow the **state (and for minimum wages, the zone) of the employee's recorded workplace**. PF is national: one establishment code, with branches usually added under it. ESI typically needs a **sub-code per state or region office** for branches. PT registration is per state where people are employed.

### Cited Findings
- **PT follows the recorded workplace.** One guide says liability "follows the location of the employee's workplace, as defined in the employment records". State of incorporation and employee residence are irrelevant, and remote work in another city does not change it unless the official location is updated. — [Treelife PT guide](https://treelife.in/?p=15607) (secondary). Contradicted in part by [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/professional-tax-india-state-wise-rates-slabs), which says PT follows the employer's registered office. I treat this as wrong or loosely worded, because Treelife, Zoho and Frappe all key PT to the work state.
- **States levying PT in FY 2026-27 (per Treelife):** Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Gujarat, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Punjab, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Tripura, West Bengal, plus Puducherry. — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607). Non-levying states include Delhi, Haryana, UP, HP and Rajasthan. — [Clevercoins](https://clevercoins.org/?p=4100); [BankBazaar](https://ndtv.bankbazaar.com/tax/professional-tax.html) (older lists that still show Odisha)
- **Odisha abolished PT** by the Odisha State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employment (Repeal) Ordinance 2026 (No. 02 of 2026), gazetted 21 Apr 2026 with effect from 1 Apr 2026. FY 2025-26 dues and the annual return remain payable. — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)
- Karnataka's PT slab was revised from 1 Apr 2025 (Karnataka Act No. 33 of 2025). — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607). Slab figures differ across blogs (a ₹15,000 vs ₹25,000 threshold). — [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/tools/professional-tax-calculator) [UNCERTAIN]
- PT is capped at ₹2,500 per person per year (Article 276). — [Clevercoins](https://clevercoins.org/?p=4100)
- **PT registration is per state:** "registration is state-specific and must be completed separately for each state in which the company employs people". There is no national registration. The source does not say whether multiple premises in one state need separate registrations. — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)
- **PT deduction frequency** varies by state: monthly, half-yearly (e.g., Tamil Nadu, Kerala) or yearly. — [Zoho Payroll setup help](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/help/employer/setup.html) (via search summary); [Frappe HR docs](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/india-payroll/professional-tax) (frequency drives calculation method)
- **LWF is state-run.** Zoho lists 16 jurisdictions with LWF: AP, Chandigarh, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Goa, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Kerala, MP, Maharashtra, Odisha, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and West Bengal. Each has its own employee/employer amounts and its own frequency (monthly, half-yearly or annual, e.g., Karnataka ₹20/₹40 yearly, deducted 31 Dec). Zoho Payroll computes LWF "based on your work location". — [Zoho LWF guide](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/taxes-and-compliance/guide-to-lwf.html). Lists conflict on Delhi: [recrew.ai](https://www.recrew.ai/glossary/labour-welfare-fund) says Delhi has no obligation. Rates differ across sources (Haryana). — [Timechamp](https://www.timechamp.io/glossary/labour-welfare-fund-lwf) [UNCERTAIN on current amounts]
- Zoho also states that LWF applies only to employees earning ₹15,000/month or less. This is a Zoho-specific simplification; thresholds vary by state. — [search summary of Zoho docs](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/help/employer/setup.html) [UNCERTAIN]
- **Working hours and registers now come from state rules under the Codes.** The notified Wages Rules mostly set 8 hours/day and 48 hours/week, with state variations: Uttarakhand allows 10 hours/day, and Rajasthan and Bihar set no daily limit. Spread-over is mostly 12 hours. The notified OSH Rules consolidate registers into an employee register, an attendance-cum-muster roll, a wages/OT/deductions register and wage slips, with a unified annual return. AP, Arunachal, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Bihar and J&K prescribe one return covering all four Codes. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- The Karnataka S&E baseline (2021 notification) is 9 hours/day and 48 hours/week, with a 10-hour cap including overtime. The proposed 10-hour day amendment was reportedly dropped in July 2025 (per a union statement). — [greytHR](https://www.greythr.com/notifications/karnataka-government-limits-work-hours-under-the-shops-and-establishments-act); [The Week, 29 Jul 2025](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/29/mes25-ka-employees-working-hours.html); [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/changes-proposed-to-the-shops-and-commercial-establishments-act-in-karnataka)
- **Minimum wages:** the Code on Wages applies minimum wages to all employees. The Centre notifies a national floor wage, and states cannot set rates below it. — [KPMG India, Dec 2025](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html). The notified state Wages Rules derive the monthly minimum as daily rate × 26 (Arunachal uses × 30). — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf). Zoho notes that each state has its own minimum wages. — [Zoho multi-state payroll](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/taxes-and-compliance/multi-state-payroll-in-india.html)
- **PF:** EPF is national and does not change with state. — [Zoho multi-state payroll](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/taxes-and-compliance/multi-state-payroll-in-india.html). Informal practitioner advice says a branch in a new state can be added under the existing PF code, but **ESI needs a new sub-code**. — [greytHR community](https://community.greythr.com/t/esic-pf-for-different-branches-in-india/1913) (forum; not authoritative). A 2011 ESIC letter describes getting a sub-code by giving the address of the nearest controlling office. — [Scribd copy of ESIC letter](https://www.scribd.com/document/758203499/ESIC-Subcode) [UNCERTAIN, old]. The Supreme Court held that once the registered office is covered by ESI, its branches are covered. — [Transport Corporation of India v. ESIC, AIR 2000 SC 238](https://cll.nliu.ac.in/?p=2043)
- New EPF Scheme 2026, EPS Scheme 2026 and EDLI Scheme 2026 were notified under the Social Security Code (29 Jun 2026). The EPF Act 1952 had not been repealed as of Dec 2025. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf); [KPMG India](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)
- ESIC is voluntary for establishments with fewer than 10 employees under the SS Code (mandatory for hazardous work). — [KPMG India](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)

### Inferences
- The Place record needs a **state** (which drives the PT, LWF, holiday and S&E/OSH rule set) and, for minimum wages, a **zone/area class**. Store the registration numbers that legally belong to the employer-in-that-state, not to each premises: PT registration (PTRC) per state, LWF per state, ESI sub-code per state or region, and S&E/OSH registration per premises. PF is one code for the company. For a small business the cleanest structure is a per-state "statutory profile" (PT no., LWF no., ESI sub-code) shared by all places in that state, with the establishment registration on the place itself.
- Rule tables (PT slabs, LWF amounts, frequency) change yearly; Odisha PT was repealed and the Karnataka slab revised in 2025. They should be data with effective dates, not code.

### Gaps
- Official state commercial-tax and labour-department pages were not fetched. PT and LWF amounts are from secondary sources and need verification before payroll use, including whether Karnataka LWF changed after Zoho's table.
- No official source was found on whether multiple premises in one state need separate PT or LWF registrations.
- I found no authoritative current EPFO or ESIC rule on branch codes; the evidence is forum and old-circular level.
- Minimum wage zone structure (e.g., Karnataka zones, Maharashtra Zone I/II/III) and the national floor-wage amount under the Code were not found **[PENDING / not researched]**.

## 3. Holiday lists by state/location in practice; restricted/optional holidays

### Takeaway
Holidays are fixed by **state** law for each establishment: a few mandatory national holidays plus festival holidays that the employer picks from a state list. Products therefore attach each holiday to one or more work locations. Karnataka's 1963 Act requires at least 10 holidays and covers shops and commercial establishments. Maharashtra's 2017 S&E Act reportedly requires 8 (4 national + 4 festival).

### Cited Findings
- The Karnataka Industrial Establishments (National and Festival Holidays) Act 1963 covers "any shop or commercial establishment" as defined in the Karnataka S&E Act 1961, as well as factories and plantations. The minimum is "10 days in a calendar year" under amended Section 3. — [greytHR wiki](https://greythr.com/wiki/acts/karnataka-industrial-establishments-national-and-festival-holidays-act-1963)
- The Karnataka split is reportedly 5 national holidays (including Karnataka Rajyotsava, 1 Nov) plus 5 festival holidays chosen by the employer. Festival-holiday pay requires 30 days' service in the preceding 90 days. Work on a holiday is paid at double wages, or a day's wage plus a substitute holiday. — [Simpliance Karnataka NFH](https://www.simpliance.in/nfh_detail/karnataka) (secondary; reported via search summary)
- Maharashtra (S&E 2017 Act, in force 19 Dec 2017) reportedly has 4 national holidays (26 Jan, 1 May, 15 Aug, 2 Oct) and 4 festival holidays agreed between employer and workers. Holiday work is paid at double wages or with a compensatory off. — [Simpliance Maharashtra NFH](https://www.simpliance.in/nfh_detail/maharashtra); [Lawgist](https://lawgist.in/states/maharashtra/maharashtra-shops-and-establishments-regulation-of-employment-and-conditions-of-service-act-2017/18) [UNCERTAIN, secondary]
- Zoho Payroll: when adding a holiday "you select the work locations where this holiday will be applicable", with national vs regional examples (Gudi Padwa for Maharashtra, Ugadi for Karnataka). — [Zoho Payroll holiday help](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/help/employer/leave-and-attendance/holiday.html)

### Inferences
- Model the holiday calendar as **per-year lists attached to places (or to states, with place override)**. Each entry is marked *national (mandatory)*, *festival (employer-chosen from the state list)* or *optional/restricted*. One calendar per state covers almost every small business. A per-place override is needed when two places in the same state choose different festivals.
- Because festival holidays are a chosen subset, the product should let the owner pick N festivals from a state list rather than imposing the full state government list. Bank or government gazetted holiday lists are much longer than what private employers must give.

### Gaps
- **Restricted/optional holidays:** I found no source in the time available. In practice these are a company policy device (borrowed from the Central Government's restricted-holiday list, where employees pick 2 from a list), not a statutory requirement for private employers. This is unverified.
- The official 2026 state notifications of national and festival holiday lists were not fetched.
- I found nothing on whether OSH Code state rules replace the state National & Festival Holidays Acts. They appear to remain separate statutes **[UNCERTAIN]**.

## 4. Multi-state small business: employee moves Karnataka → Maharashtra

### Takeaway
When the recorded workplace changes, the following switch on that date: PT (Karnataka slab → Maharashtra slab, under Maharashtra registration), LWF (Karnataka annual → Maharashtra half-yearly), the holiday calendar, the governing S&E/OSH state rules, and the minimum wage schedule. PF stays the same. ESI coverage continues, but contributions may need filing under the Maharashtra sub-code. The employer needs PT (and probably LWF) registrations in Maharashtra if it has none.

### Cited Findings
- Frappe HR handles this by creating a new Salary Structure Assignment with a new **Employment State**, so each assignment gets the correct PT rule. — [Frappe HR docs](https://docs.frappe.io/hr/india-payroll/professional-tax)
- A payroll blog works through a Karnataka→Maharashtra transfer: Karnataka rules up to the move, Maharashtra rules after, with the Feb/March amounts handled under Maharashtra's rules (illustrative figures). — [Omnivoo PT blog](https://omnivoo.com/blog/professional-tax-india) (secondary; search summary)
- PT is capped at ₹2,500 per person per year, so combined deductions across both states should be checked against the cap. A SAP case shows retro payroll runs double-counting after a state move. — [Clevercoins](https://clevercoins.org/?p=4100); [SAP Community](https://answers.sap.com/questions/12537315/professional-tax-not-getting-deducted.html) [UNCERTAIN whether the cap is enforced across two states' separate Acts]
- PT registration must be obtained in each state where the company employs people. — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)
- Remote work alone does not change the PT state; the official work location must be updated. — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)
- Zoho applies state-specific PT and LWF rules automatically from the assigned work location, but does not document mid-cycle transfer handling. — [Zoho multi-state payroll](https://www.zoho.com/in/payroll/academy/taxes-and-compliance/multi-state-payroll-in-india.html)
- If the Maharashtra premises has fewer than 10 workers, it gives S&E commencement intimation (Section 7). At 10 or more, it needs OSH Code registration, and no separate S&E registration is required. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- The employee's work place must be **effective-dated** (history of place assignments with a from-date), because PT, LWF and holiday entitlement are computed per period. A transfer screen should show the user what changes, e.g., "PT moves to Maharashtra from 1 Nov; holiday calendar changes to Maharashtra 2026", and flag a missing Maharashtra PT registration.
- Opening a first employee in a new state is a compliance event for the business (PT and LWF registration, ESI sub-code, establishment intimation or registration). For a small business, a checklist triggered by "first person in a new state" is worth more than any modelling depth.

### Gaps
- No official rule was found on proration of PT in the month of transfer, or on Maharashtra's February ₹300 adjustment interplay. Verify with the state Acts.
- Festival-holiday eligibility after a transfer (e.g., the 30-of-90-days service condition in Karnataka) was not researched.

## 5. 2025–2026 changes relevant to place modelling (Labour Codes status, Karnataka S&E)

### Takeaway
All four Labour Codes took effect on **21 Nov 2025**. The Central Rules were notified on **8–9 May 2026**. State rules are partial: only some states have notified final rules under each Code. In 2026, several states (Maharashtra, Haryana, Karnataka, A&N) removed separate S&E registration for OSH Code-registered establishments, Bihar repealed its S&E Act, and Assam amended its own. Establishment registration is moving from S&E to the OSH Code at different speeds in each state, and some notification is still pending.

### Cited Findings
- The four Codes (Wages, IR, Social Security, OSH) took effect on 21 Nov 2025, with some provisions not yet commenced. — [EY India alert](https://www.ey.com/en_in/technical/alerts-hub/2025/11/new-labour-codes-implemented-across-the-country-effective-21-november-2025); [BDO India](https://www.bdo.in/en-gb/insights/alerts-updates/alert-implementation-of-labour-codes-key-provisions-notified-effective-21-november-2025)
- As of Dec 2025, final Central and State rules were not notified, and existing rules continued where they did not conflict with the Codes. — [KPMG India](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html)
- Central Rules under all four Codes, plus Model Standing Orders 2026, were notified on 8 May 2026. The OSH (Central) Rules are dated 8 May 2026 per AMS, or 9 May per one search summary of other sources. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf); [KPMG flash news](https://www.in.kpmg.com/taxflashnews/KPMG-Flash-News-Notification-of-final-Rules-on-Four-Labour-Codes.pdf)
- States with final rules as of 1 Oct 2026, per AMS:
  - **Wages Code:** AP, Arunachal, Bihar, Gujarat, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, UP.
  - **OSH Code:** AP, Arunachal, Bihar, Gujarat, J&K, Ladakh, Lakshadweep, Rajasthan, UP.
  - Karnataka and Maharashtra are **not** in AMS's OSH-rules list.

  This conflicts with an iPleaders blog (May 2026) that says Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka had notified rules under all four Codes. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf); contradicted by [iPleaders](https://blog.ipleaders.in/labour-codes-2026-implementation/) (blog unreachable, HTTP 521; claim known only via search summary) [UNCERTAIN; AMS is the more authoritative and more recent source]
- The Karnataka S&E (Amendment) Act 2026 (reported 4 Sep 2026) exempts OSH-registered establishments with 10+ workers and modernises compliance. Employers should continue S&E compliance until OSH registration is operational in Karnataka **[PENDING]**. — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- The 2025 Karnataka proposal for a 10-hour day (raising quarterly overtime from 50 to 144 hours, and exempting businesses under 10 employees) was reportedly dropped in July 2025. — [DLA Piper](https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/changes-proposed-to-the-shops-and-commercial-establishments-act-in-karnataka); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/07/29/mes25-ka-employees-working-hours.html)
- The Codes require appointment letters for all employees, leave encashment on demand above 30 days of carried-forward leave, and a national floor wage. — [KPMG India](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2025/12/implementation-of-labour-codes-what-changes-and-road-ahead.html). Several notified OSH Rules prescribe an appointment-letter format that includes "establishment details". — [AMS Newsletter Oct 2026](https://www.amsshardul.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ELB-Newsletter-October-2026.pdf)
- Odisha PT was repealed with effect from 1 Apr 2026, and the Karnataka PT slab revised from 1 Apr 2025. — [Treelife](https://treelife.in/?p=15607)

### Inferences
- The OSH Code's 10-worker threshold, which recurs in the Maharashtra, Karnataka, Assam and A&N changes, is a natural trigger the product can show on a place: "under 10 people: intimation only; 10 or more: OSH Code registration".
- Because appointment letters must carry establishment details, the place (establishment name, address and registration number) should be printable on employee documents.
- For 2026, the safest product stance is to store registration numbers and types as plain owner-entered data and avoid hard-coding which Act applies in which state.

### Gaps
- Final state rules for Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Telangana and Delhi under each Code could not be confirmed. AMS and iPleaders conflict.
- The national floor-wage amount, and whether it has been notified, were not found.
- Whether state National & Festival Holidays Acts are affected by the Codes was not found.
