# People: the plan after the 8 November deep research

Four read-only research passes on main at 0ef7ca24 (8 Nov 2026 evening release): navigation across ten HR products, every workflow traced through the code, the reports catalogue against six products and Indian statutory registers, and a 46-screen visual audit of the running app. This file is the agreed plan. It supersedes `04-WORDS-AND-MENUS.md` §3 for the menu and feeds the build order in the 8 Nov People plan.

Owner decisions carried forward: rail Home · Inbox · People · Time · Workplace · Talent (when on) · Reports · Settings; Organisation leaves the rail; one Inbox with Approve per row; sentence case; plain words; page title equals menu label. Owner accepted this plan on 8 Nov ("yes start"), including Workplace as the name and Home as the employee's own page.

## 1. What is wrong, in one paragraph each

**Navigation.** The words are mostly right; the placement is not. Organisation is still a rail module (no product reviewed has one). Approvals-related screens (Delegates) sit under Leave. Settings is 35 flat screens. Reports mixes reports with a to-do list (Compliance) and an auditor view. Two screens are named the same as their group. "Growth" is vague where every product says "Performance".

**Frontend.** The frame (rail, panel, icons, top bar) scores 7/10; the work area 4–5/10. Five causes: screens contradict each other (Home "nothing waits" next to 320 tasks waiting and an empty Inbox); empty states are dead ends (same icon on ~25 screens, primaries that open forms that cannot be completed); a ~280px right column of filler on ~20 screens; five different list header and toolbar layouts; brand colour used for titles, column heads and data bars so nothing stands out.

**Workflows.** The engine, outbox, change log, payroll bridge and employee app are solid. Leave, attendance fixes, shift swaps, expenses, profile changes, help desk and delegates are complete. What breaks the feeling of a product is the hand-offs: hire does not create the salary assignment, send the joining form or file the offer; resignation does not store the resignation date so notice pay is always ₹0; letters are never issued by an event; four approval types exist in Approval rules but nothing creates them; managers never see overtime in the Inbox; every notice is bell-only, no e-mail.

**Reports.** One real analytic (headcount and attrition, as a table with no filters and no chart). The best People reports exist in the Attendance app (birthdays and milestones, expiring documents, attendance summary, late and absent, overtime, scheduled e-mail) and People does not link to them. The register of employees omits leavers. No tenure, gender, probation, joiners-and-leavers list, hiring funnel or ratings reports.

## 3. Frontend rules that fix the repeating problems

1. One count, one truth: Home's subtitle, the Waiting card, the Inbox badge and the bell all read the same server number.
2. One list header: breadcrumb, title, description, one primary top right, toolbar on one line (search · filters · view), tabs with counts, then the list. No figure strip above tabs that carry the same counts.
3. No right column unless it carries data the page does not already show. Today's date, quick links and "nothing pending" go.
4. Empty state per job: what this screen is for, one action that can be completed. A calendar always draws its month; an org chart with no reporting lines opens grouped by department at 100%.
5. Brand colour is for actions only. Titles, section heads and column heads in ink or muted ink; data bars in a neutral or the series colours.
6. One segmented-control style (solid teal), one status pill, two radii, nothing under 12px, 44px tap targets on a phone.
7. Formatted values everywhere: "31 Dec 2023", "Female", ₹ without paise on totals.
8. Each Settings row has its own glyph; no icon reused for unrelated things.

## 4. Build order

Each step is one PR, browser-tested, with tests. Server changes carry the `full-tests` label.

| # | Step | Size |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Menu:** the tree above in `manifest.jsx`, Settings in 7 groups, Reports in 4 groups, renames, removals, `04-WORDS-AND-MENUS.md` rewritten, navigation tests updated | M |
| 2 | **Home truth and Inbox:** one waiting count end to end; joining and leaving tasks appear in Inbox › My tasks; overtime and comp-off reach the step's approver whatever their role | M |
| 3 | **List header and empty states:** shared header component applied to every People list; right column removed where it is filler; dead-end empty states replaced (leave types, revisions, calendar grid, org chart) | L |
| 4 | **Workflow hand-offs, hiring side:** hire requires an accepted offer or an explicit choice; outside hire sends the joining form and app invite, files the offer, drafts the Payroll salary assignment from the offer | M |
| 5 | **Workflow hand-offs, leaving side:** resignation stores its date; notice owed, served and shortfall flow into the F&F notice line; clearance money and unreturned assets become F&F lines | M |
| 6 | **Letters by event:** appointment on hire, confirmation on confirm, increment on revision applied, relieving and experience on separation; each ticks its checklist task; employee asks for a letter in the app (LETTER_REQUEST and DOCUMENT_REQUEST get a creator and an effect, DESK_ESCALATION and SEAT_REQUEST removed from Approval rules) | L |
| 7 | **E-mail for approvals and decisions**, plus employee-app notices, from `notifyStep` and `tellRequester` | M |
| 8 | **Reports, People group:** headcount & attrition with filters and a chart (fix `placeNow`), joiners & leavers list, probation & confirmations, team make-up; birthdays and expiring documents re-hosted from Attendance | L |
| 9 | **Reports, Time and registers:** attendance summary, late/early/absent, overtime re-hosted; leave balances as on a date; register of employees with leavers, exit date and real UAN/ESIC; one muster-roll service; scheduled e-mail for People reports | L |
| 10 | **Remaining workflow gaps:** review cycle tells employees on open and shared, ratings can seed a revision; promotion offers "record revision"; rehire restarts the Payroll profile; probation confirms on the end date with a lapse reminder; comp-off expiry; announcement notify and read receipt | M |
| 11 | **Phone pass:** chrome before content on Home and Employees, tap targets, attendance row identity | M |
| 12 | **Statutory extras** (later, with a CA where noted): statutory bonus, minimum-wage warning, maternity and accident registers, POSH annual report, Form 11, gratuity nomination | L |

Steps 1–3 are the frontend the owner sees first. Steps 4–7 are the workflow fixes. Steps 8–9 are reports. 10–12 close the rest.

**Progress.** Steps 1–4 merged by 10 Nov. Step 5 (10 Nov): the final settlement's notice line is worked out from the resignation date and the employment type's notice days (shortfall × gross ÷ 30, nothing when waived); money a department records on the clearance sign-off becomes its own line; a register asset still held becomes a ₹0 line for HR to price. Accounts' clearance figure is not added again, because advances, loans and claims already come from their records.

**Progress, step 6 (10 Nov):** a company's own appointment, confirmation, increment, relieving and experience letters now go out by themselves on hire, confirm, pay rise and leaving (once each, signed by Settings › Letters › Signed by, ticking their plan step); employees ask for a letter or a document copy in the app's Me tab, and approval issues the letter or gives HR a task; help desk escalations and seat requests left Approval rules.
