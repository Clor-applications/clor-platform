# Six company settings: defaults

Owner, 31 Oct 2026: each pending decision becomes a setting that HR and management choose. Details, law and code are in `SPEC.md` section 3.
Every change is written to the audit trail with before and after values. Settings 1 and 4 also keep a dated version, so a month or settlement already worked out keeps the rule it used.

| # | Setting (as the screen says it) | Choices | Default | Who may change it | Today |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Late marks cut **[the time actually missed]**, after **[10]** min grace, the first **[N]** a month free | Time actually missed · Time missed, rounded up to 15/30/60 min · Every N marks cost ½ or 1 day | **Time actually missed** for companies not yet using late marks; companies already using them keep **every N marks = ½ day** with their numbers | HR with company-wide scope, owner | Every N marks = ½ or 1 day; off by default |
| 2 | Attendance requests (missed punch, WFH, on duty, permission, shift change) are decided by | HR or the manager, whoever is first · The manager (HR steps in when there is none) · HR only · The manager, then HR | **HR or the manager, whoever is first** (what today's "HR" really does) | Whoever edits approval rules, owner | Chain "HR", with team approvers also deciding |
| 3 | HR without Payroll access can see salary revisions (read only) | No · Yes | **No** | Owner or admin only | No |

Final wages: days worked, notice pay owed, leave encashment, other earnings (not gratuity, not reimbursements) | Cap 0–50% · Final wages components (the rest always stays owed) | **50%, rest stays owed**, components as listed | Payroll settings editors, owner | Recovered in full, no cap |
| 5 | Read-only users and IT administrators see who is leaving and the last day / also the reason typed | Yes/No · Yes/No | **Leavings: yes. Reason: no.** | Owner or admin only | Both see leavings and the reason |
| 6 | When someone withdraws a request, the "waiting for you" notice | Shows as withdrawn · Is removed | **Shows as withdrawn** (marked read, links to the closed request) | Whoever edits approval rules, owner | Stays as "waiting for you" |

Reasons, in short:

1. Code on Wages s.20 allows a cut only in proportion to the time missed, so time missed is the safe default. Companies already using the ½-day rule are not changed under their feet.
2. This keeps today's behaviour and names it honestly.
3. Pay stays private to Payroll and the owner.
4. Code on Wages s.18(3) caps deductions at half the wages for the wage period. The final settlement had no cap.
5. A reason for leaving is often personal.
6. An approver should see why a request disappeared.

Owner answers (31 Oct 2026, in chat), now decided:

- **Q1.** The half-day late-mark mode stays, with the warning and the per-person comparison.
- **Q2.** Final settlements use a 50% cap with the rest left owed. There is no consent option.
- **Q3.** Leave encashment counts as final wages.
