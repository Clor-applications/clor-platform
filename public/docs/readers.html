Readers

Every make ends in the same place: a punch with a time, a person and a direction. What differs is how it reaches CLOR.

Direction (in / out)

Readers with in/out keys send the key pressed. A reader at an entrance only can be set to “Entry only” so every punch is an entry. Where a reader says nothing, the first punch of the day is in and the last is out.

Clocks

CLOR measures each reader's drift from punches it sends as they happen. ADMS readers get a “Set the time” button on their page; Matrix controllers and readers behind the on-site connector are set automatically when a minute or more out. Reader health warns when a clock is out.

Health and alerts

Attendance → Readers → Reader health shows every reader, how long since it was heard, its clock, how full it is and what is queued. Name who gets e-mailed under Alerts; a reader that goes quiet, a day with no punches, a connector that stops checking in — each is one e-mail when it starts and one when it clears.
